Dray tells you when a session hands work back to you. It picks one way to tell you, based on where you are, so you never get the same news twice.

## What Dray tells you about

- **Done** — a session's turn ended.
- **Needs permission** — a session wants your approval before it runs a tool.
- **Needs an answer** — a session asked you a question.
- **Ready to merge** — a session's pull request has turned ready.

## Which way you hear

For the first three, the channel depends on where you are:

| You are | You get |
|---|---|
| In another app | A macOS banner, with the session's title. Clicking it brings Dray forward on that session. |
| In Dray, looking at something else | A card in the top-left corner of the window, and a sound. |
| In Dray, looking at that session | Nothing. The transcript already shows it. |

**Ready to merge** works differently. It is always a card, never a banner, even when you are looking at the session — the panel line changing quietly is easy to miss. It appears when a PR changes to ready, not for one that was already ready when Dray first saw it.

Sessions filed in another [space](/using-dray/spaces) stay silent until you switch to that space. They keep running.

## Cards

Each card names what is wanted and offers a button:

| Card | Button | Goes to | Stays for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Done | View | The session | 10 seconds |
| Needs permission, Needs an answer | Answer | The session, where the card to answer is | 15 seconds |
| Ready to merge | Review | The session with its PR tab open | 10 seconds |
| Delete worktree? | Delete or Skip | Delete removes the worktree of a session you just settled. Skip keeps it. | 15 seconds |

The bar along the bottom of a card is its timer. Hover a card to pause it.

### Keyboard

- `⌘G` takes the top card — the oldest one, next to expire. Press it again for the next. On a **Delete worktree?** card it skips; it never deletes.
- `⌘⇧D` deletes the worktree from a **Delete worktree?** card.

The keys work only while a card is showing. Hover the top card's button to see its key.

A card going away does not mean the work is done. A session still waiting on you keeps its yellow mark in the sidebar until you answer.

## Sounds

- An in-app card plays a short sound.
- A banner for a finished task plays the macOS default sound. A banner asking for permission or an answer plays Ping.
- Settling a session plays a short celebration.

There is no setting to turn these off.

## Sidebar marks and the dock badge

Each sidebar row carries a mark on its left edge:

- **Green** — the turn is over and you haven't looked yet. Opening the row clears it.
- **Yellow** — the session is waiting on you. Only answering clears it.

Yellow wins when both apply. The Dray icon in the Dock shows a count of sessions with either mark, across every space, and clears itself at zero.

## Allow notifications in macOS

macOS asks whether Dray may send notifications the first time it posts a banner. Choose **Allow**. If you declined, turn it back on in System Settings → Notifications → Dray. Cards, sounds, marks and the badge inside Dray work either way.

## Limits

- A banner disappears after a minute if you don't click it.
- A background subagent that reports back after its turn can raise a second **Done** for the same session.
- Shortcuts here can be changed in Settings → Shortcuts.
